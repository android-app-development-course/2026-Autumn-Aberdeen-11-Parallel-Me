# Parallel Me | Life Exploration Lab

> Before you change your life, try another one.

## 1. Project Overview

Parallel Me is a proposed mobile application that helps adults explore alternative life possibilities before making major life changes.

The app aims to help users rediscover deferred ambitions, explore possible futures, learn from real people's experiences, and test new identities through small, structured life experiments.

Parallel Me is designed to help users make informed choices about their lives. It does not encourage users to abandon their existing responsibilities or make drastic decisions without reflection.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many adults have established careers, families, and responsibilities, but may have postponed personal ambitions or alternative life paths.

Traditional productivity, learning, and social applications address specific needs, while Parallel Me proposes a connected journey from self-discovery to real-world experimentation.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Parallel Me combines guided self-reflection, AI-assisted future scenarios, real-world observation, and short life experiments.

Its goal is to help users discover what matters to them and identify realistic next steps, including small changes to their current lives.

## 4. Core Product System

### 4.1 The Unfinished Self

Guided reflection helps users identify ambitions, interests, and possible identities they have not had the opportunity to explore. The proposed output is an editable Unfinished Self Map.

### 4.2 Life Rehearsal

Users explore alternative future scenarios and compare their assumptions, trade-offs, and possible consequences. AI-generated scenarios are exploratory possibilities, not predictions.

### 4.3 Borrow My Life

Users may observe aspects of another person's real working or everyday life through carefully managed experiences. This feature requires appropriate consent, screening, and safety procedures before launch.

### 4.4 Parallel Life Mission

Users undertake short, structured experiments, such as a 24-hour, three-day, or seven-day activity, and reflect on what they learn.

Together, these stages form a continuous cycle:

**Remember → Imagine → Observe → Experience → Reflect → Redesign**

## 5. Unique Selling Proposition

Parallel Me aims to connect self-discovery, alternative-future scenarios, real-world observation, and practical experiments in one user-controlled journey.

Unlike products focused primarily on productivity or content consumption, Parallel Me proposes to help users test a possible life before making a major commitment.

## 6. Target Users

The initial target-user hypothesis is adults aged approximately 40–55 who have stable responsibilities but want to revisit a deferred ambition or explore another possible identity.

The initial research will particularly consider midlife women, while remaining open to other adults navigating career, lifestyle, or personal transitions.

This target segment is a hypothesis to validate through user research.

## 7. Proposed Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The initial product validation scope may include:

- Guided self-discovery
- An editable Unfinished Self Map
- A limited set of alternative-future scenarios
- A structured reflection log
- A small life-mission planner

A broad real-world experience marketplace is not part of the initial MVP until its operational and safety requirements are addressed.
